# Product Spec

Pandharkawda Arogya is a mobile-first bilingual civic healthcare navigation product for residents, families, visitors, senior citizens, doctors, clinics, laboratories, public hospital administrators, verifiers, medical content reviewers, and platform administrators in Pandharkawda, Yavatmal District, Maharashtra.

It is not a diagnosis product and does not replace professional medical care. Its job is to help people discover verified local healthcare information quickly, understand basic preparation and scheme information, and reach emergency services with very low friction.

## Public Jobs

- Find a relevant doctor, facility, scheme, test, procedure, or health alert.
- Understand if a facility is open separately from whether a doctor is available.
- View confirmed visiting specialist sessions.
- Call, navigate, save locally, or report incorrect information.
- Ask Arogya for grounded help from verified local data or reviewed content.
- Access emergency help from every important public page.

## Trust Rules

Only `VERIFIED` records are public by default. `STALE` records either remain hidden or show a strong "Please call to confirm" warning. Unknown facts are displayed as unknown; demo records are labelled fictional.

## Languages

English and Marathi are supported through translation keys and persisted client-side language preference. Emergency strings are curated static translations.
